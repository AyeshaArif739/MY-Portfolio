About me

I'm currently studying an AI specialization in Web Designing & Development at Aptech Computer Education, alongside a cybersecurity course at Banoqabil and my HSC in Computer Science. Most of what I know about building for the web, I learned by finishing one project and immediately wanting the next one to do more.

That's the throughline across the six sites here: MindSprint taught me structure, SafeCarz earned Project of the Month for how it looked and worked, and CookSmart was my first time wiring a real backend to a React frontend. I like design and development equally — Figma and Photoshop sit next to VS Code in my toolkit, not apart from it.

Outside of assignments, my actual hobby is signing up for a new course the moment I finish one — I completed a Graphic Designing CIT course this way and I'm currently working through Cybersecurity Essentials for the same reason. Once I start something, I like finishing it properly before I move on to the next.
